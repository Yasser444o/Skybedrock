## Mechanics
- You can obtain custom maps from abandoned camps
- Those maps will lead you to find areas with biomes outside the biomes map
- They also contain maps that lead to structures like Ancient City and Mineshaft and Abandoned Camps
- Those maps work like cartographer maps
- Maps for biomes can be obtained from the suspicious gravel
- Maps for structures can be obtained from fishing crates
- Jungle Cartographers can sell maps for jungle abandoned camps
