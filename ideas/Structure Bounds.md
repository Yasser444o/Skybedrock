## The Addon
- Structure Bounds is a standalone addon that is added to skybedrock by default
- It gives you a few commands that leverage the getGeneratedStructures API for visualizing structure boundaries

## Commands
- `/scan_structures` Finds all the structures in the loaded chunks (sim 4 chunks)
- `/trace_structure` It draws the bounding boxes of the structure at your xz location
- `/read_structure` Lists the structures you are currently inside

## Integration
- It can detect skybedrock trial chambers for visualizing where trial vaults and spawners can be placed
- It has the bounding box of the nether fortress saved at initialization
