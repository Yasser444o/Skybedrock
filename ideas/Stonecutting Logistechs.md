## Mechanics
- Mason Zombie Villagers can use pickaxes
- They will chisel the blocks in front of them into different variants
- Pickaxe durability isn't consumed
- The variant depends on which redstone signal is going to that block
- They can do all stonecutting recipes which give one item
- Pistons can place the raw variants of the blocks which can be chiseled
- Complex recipes like Deepslate Brick Wall are done in multiple steps: Deepslate > Cobble > Bricks > Wall
